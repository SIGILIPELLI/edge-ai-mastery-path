# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-23_

This page covers **Edge AI Mastery Path**, part of [Mastery Path](https://learnmasterypath.com/). The full policy for every Mastery Path site is at [learnmasterypath.com/privacy.html](https://learnmasterypath.com/privacy.html).

## Cookies, ads and analytics

This site shows ads through **Google AdSense**. Google and its partners may use cookies or device identifiers to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads in [Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) or at [aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

**Google Analytics** measures overall traffic (pages visited, time on page, general device and location). You can opt out with the [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## What stays in your browser

Lesson ticks, quiz scores, notes and your saved name are stored only in your browser's local storage. They are not sent to us, and clearing this site's data removes them.

## Optional email sign-up

If you enter your email to get the free Interview Prep Pack, we store the email, the page you signed up from and the date, and use it only for occasional Mastery Path updates. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

## Optional certification exam

If you take the certification exam on the Final Quiz page, your answers are marked on our server. If you pass, we store the name you entered, the course, your score and the date under a certificate ID, so anyone you share the ID with can check it at [learnmasterypath.com/verify](https://learnmasterypath.com/verify/). Certificates are issued by Mastery Path and are not accredited qualifications.

## What this site does not do

No account is needed and every lesson is free to read. We do not sell personal information.

## Contact

Write to [contact@learnmasterypath.com](mailto:contact@learnmasterypath.com) to ask a question, or to have your email address or a certificate record deleted.
